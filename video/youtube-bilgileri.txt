=============================== ENGLISH VIDEO (kayra-tanitim-en.mp4) ===============================

TITLE:
KAYRA – Your own JARVIS: an open-source voice AI assistant written in C++

DESCRIPTION:
Kayra is a voice assistant for Windows inspired by Tony Stark's JARVIS. Say "Kayra" and ask: it tells you the time, the weather and facts from Wikipedia; opens apps and sites, searches YouTube, sets reminders, does math and remembers you. Speech recognition runs entirely on your computer (whisper.cpp); your voice never leaves it. Optionally connect Ollama, LM Studio, Kor Studio, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter as its brain for free conversation.

Every shot of the app in this video is a real recording of the app itself, in Kayra's own voice.
